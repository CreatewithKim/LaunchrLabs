# LaunchrLabs Africa Content and Asset Register

> **Purpose:** This working register supports the content and copyright hygiene recommendations in the supplied *Launchr Labs Website Content & Copyright Improvement Audit*. It is not legal advice, a license grant, or a substitute for owner review.

## Content originality record

The positioning, service descriptions, calls to action, process language, and metadata in this project were rewritten for LaunchrLabs Africa around a bottleneck-first message architecture. The guiding promise is that Launchr builds systems that make businesses easier to run and easier to grow. Before external campaigns or case-study publication, the brand owner should review every factual claim and retain approval records.

## Proof and claims policy

The website does not publish invented testimonials, ratings, client results, or performance metrics. Add case studies only with permission-based facts, approved screenshots, source data, and a documented client approval. Each future claim should explain the business problem, system delivered, and verified change rather than relying on generic praise.

## Release checklist

- Confirm a brand owner has approved the final page copy and CTA language.
- Confirm every third-party asset, font, icon, and image has a clear source or license record.
- Keep approval records for client names, screenshots, metrics, testimonials, and case-study narratives.
- Recheck generated visual assets for accidental similarity to recognisable third-party work before paid distribution.
