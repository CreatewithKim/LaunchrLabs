# LaunchrLabs Africa — Website Design Direction

## Three Initial Directions

### 1. Momentum System
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast B2B growth platform with sharp type, strategic green accents, and product-like interface fragments. It feels decisive, modern, and built for founders who value momentum.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial African innovation journal with warm materials, documentary imagery, and calm modular storytelling. It feels credible, regional, and community-led.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Signal Studio
**Very Brief Intro:** A minimal studio presentation that treats every page as a considered brand statement, using generous whitespace and abstract directional graphics. It feels exacting, premium, and quietly ambitious.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction — Momentum System

### Design Movement
**Contemporary SaaS editorialism**: a polished Launchr SaaS-derived visual language that combines product-interface clarity with an assertive editorial rhythm. It establishes LaunchrLabs Africa as a serious partner for ambitious teams rather than a generic agency or a one-page campaign.

### Core Principles
1. **Forward motion over decoration:** angled line-work, directional crops, and staggered content structures signal progress without relying on visual noise.
2. **Authority through restraint:** large, confident headings; controlled color; and carefully placed detail create a trustworthy, investor-ready tone.
3. **Product logic in a service website:** outcomes, programmes, and ways to engage are communicated with the clarity of a well-designed SaaS interface.
4. **Regional specificity without visual cliché:** the site foregrounds African ambition through its content, people, and opportunities, not through overused decorative motifs.

### Color Philosophy
The visual foundation is deep near-black and soft mineral white: a high-contrast field that keeps information legible and makes every decision feel intentional. An electric Launchr green becomes the **signature brand color**, reserved for moments of action, momentum markers, active states, and key programme cues. Soft graphite and warm grey create depth around the core palette. This echoes the existing Launchr SaaS brand while allowing Africa-focused content to feel warm and grounded rather than cold or corporate.

### Layout Paradigm
The site follows a **guided journey** rather than a centered landing-page stack. A slim vertical momentum rail, asymmetrical section starts, and alternating full-bleed / contained content zones create the sense of moving through a well-curated operating system. Primary pages use a wide editorial canvas with an anchored information column; mobile resolves this into a natural linear reading sequence.

### Signature Elements
1. **Momentum rail:** a thin green progress line and compact numbered section tags used on key long-form sections.
2. **Orbit marks:** subtly rotating or drifting circular line motifs behind selected hero and CTA compositions.
3. **System labels:** uppercase, letter-spaced micro-labels such as “01 / What we build” and “Programme / Founders”, used sparingly to anchor hierarchy.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should reward intent, not distract from it. Cards lift a few pixels and reveal a directional arrow; buttons compress slightly on press; nav links gain a fine green underline. The booking route remains immediate and unambiguous, available from every major navigation context and with no unnecessary form step introduced by the website.

### Animation
Use 160–260 ms `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` transitions for hover, focus, and navigation state. On page entry, section labels and text may rise 12 px and fade in with a restrained 40–60 ms stagger. The orbit mark moves only at a nearly imperceptible speed; all nonessential animation is disabled for reduced-motion preferences. No looping attention-grabbing effects, parallax, or layout-shifting animations.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for display headings and buttons, paired with **DM Sans** for body text and utility labels. Headlines use tightly tracked, bold to semi-bold text with deliberate line breaks; body copy remains open and readable at 16–18 px. Micro-labels use DM Sans uppercase at small size with generous tracking. The brand never uses Inter as its default.

### Brand Essence
**LaunchrLabs Africa is the operating partner for African founders and organisations ready to turn ambitious growth into measurable momentum.**

**Personality:** decisive, collaborative, future-facing.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and outcome-led. Calls to action are specific, useful, and never inflated; microcopy explains the next step in plain language.

> “Turn the next growth decision into real momentum.”

> “Book a working session — bring the opportunity, leave with direction.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The logo is a compact directional “L” mark built from a split rising vector and circular launch point. It is always used as a graphic symbol alongside a custom spaced LaunchrLabs wordmark, never as default-font text. The symbol should remain recognizable at favicon scale.

### Signature Brand Color
**Launch Green — `#B7F53A`**. It is a high-energy signal colour used intentionally against the near-black foundation.

## Style Decisions

- **Launch Green `#B7F53A` is reserved for primary actions, rails, active states, key numerals, and rare signature CTA fields.** A full green section may appear at most once per route and must carry dark directional momentum geometry.
- **Every primary route includes a visible momentum system.** Numbered section labels work together with a persistent route rail, directional linework, or sequenced interface cue so the journey never reads as a simple stacked brochure.
- **Imagery is treated as part of the system.** African founder and business moments are framed with dark crops, Launch Green signal accents, directional overlays, or product-interface labels; unmodified generic office imagery is avoided.
